Privacy Policy — Story Stop Publisher

Last updated: August 2026

Story Stop Publisher ("the App") is a personal automation tool operated by Boluwatife Adesioye ("I", "me"). The App is used solely to publish video content that I create to my own TikTok account. It is not a public product, and it is not made available to other users.

What information the App accesses

The App connects to my own TikTok account via TikTok's official Content Posting API, using OAuth authorization that I grant myself. It accesses only the minimum scopes required to upload video content on my behalf (video.upload). The App does not access, read, or store any other TikTok user's data, and it does not collect data from anyone other than the account owner.

What information the App stores

The App stores an OAuth access token and refresh token locally, used solely to authenticate requests to TikTok's API on my own behalf. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Data retention

Locally stored tokens are retained only for as long as needed to operate the App and are deleted if the App is uninstalled or the TikTok account is disconnected.
